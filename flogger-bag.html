Flogger Bags

Protect your flogger investment from nicks, dust, and tangles in your toy bag or hanging in your dungeon! Our cotton-poly blend Flogger Bags are well constructed with finished, non-raveling interior seams for many years of use.

Dual nylon drawstrings at each end of the bag ensures quick and easy opening, as well as a very secure closure.

For use, simply insert flogger handle into either end of the Flogger Bag, slide the bag down over handle and falls of your flogger. Pull the drawstring closed at the bottom, and pull top drawstring tightly around the base of the handle, leaving the lanyard protruding from the top end of the Flogger Bag.

All Flogger Bags are 4" wide by 33" length.
