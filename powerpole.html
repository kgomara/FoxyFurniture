Power Pole

Hide this bondage/whipping post in plain sight in your living room as a coffee table and coat tree.

The coffee table measures 40" square and is 18" high. The coat tree stands 6' 6" off the floor. Both are very functional in their household roles.

For play, there are four spreader bars: two 30", one 24", and one 18". You can also make more in different lengths. The spreader bars slide through holes in the pole at a variety of heights and store in the framework of the table/platform when not in use.

Converting from furniture to play station.

Power Pole's legs detach with a few knobs, and the hardware stashes underneath the tabletop. Similarly, the coat rack disassembles so the pole can be attached to the tabletop base.

Use the left and right arrows to scroll through the examples and check it out!

To convert from coffee table and coat rack...

Unscrew the knobs and remove the pole from the coat rack,

flip the table up to rest on its side,

remove the table legs,

insert the pole into the base,

use the knobs to attach the pole to the base,

slide spreader bars into the desired holes,

and attach your play partner!
