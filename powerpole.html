Power Pole

Hide this Bondage/Whipping Post in plain sight in your living room as a Coffee Table and Coat Tree.

The Coffee Table measures 40" square and is 18" high. The Coat Tree stands 6' 6" off the floor. Both are very functional in their household roles.

For play purposes there are 4 Spreader Bars - two 30", one 24", and one 18", and of course you can easily make more of different lengths. The Spreader Bars slide through holes in the Pole in any of numerous locations and are stored in the framework of the table/platform when not in use.

This piece of equipment supports up to 250 lbs.

Converting from furniture to play station.

Power Pole's legs detach with a few knobs and the hardware is stashed underneath the table top. Similarly the coat rack disassembles so the pole can be attached to the (table-top) base.

Use the left and right arrows so scroll through the examples and check it out!

To convert from coffee table and coat rack...

Unscrew knobs and remove pole from coat rack,

Flip table up to rest on side,

Remove table legs,

Insert pole into base,

Use knobs to attach pole to base,

Slide spreader bars into desired holes,

And attach play partner!
