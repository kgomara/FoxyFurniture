Hidden in Plain Sight

Power Pole

Hide this bondage/whipping post in plain sight in your living room as a coffee table and coat tree.

The coffee table is 40" square and 18" high. The coat tree stands 6' 6". Both are very functional in their household roles.

Spreader bars slide through holes in the pole at a variety of heights and store in the table's framework.
