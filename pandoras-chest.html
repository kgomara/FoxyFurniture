Pandora's Chest

It's a lovely hope chest that easily converts to one of several play positions in a few minutes without tools.

Pandora's Chest is 20" tall (26" including optional bolsters), 44" wide, and 18" deep. The linen storage area is approximately 4 cubic feet.

The plans include two styles: Mission (shown here) or Raised Panel, shown on the Gallery page.
