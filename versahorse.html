stand it upright,

spread sides and attach arm/leg pads.

Stock can be used alone,

or as Pillory:

mount Pillory attachment to Base VersaHorse,

and attach Stock.

To use Pegasus Bench:

mount wings on each side,

and slide outriggers through eyebolts into blocks.

VersaHorse System

We call it VersaHorse to symbolize the versatility of this classic bondage horse design. Free plans include:

  • VersaHorse Base
  • Stock
  • Pillory
  • Pegasus Bench

For additional pictures see the Gallery page.

VersaHorse Base

Start with base VersaHorse. Tie-down straps secure your partner's limbs and back, holding them in position.

Stock

Stocks date back to medieval times. Foxy Furniture's is a classic design with holes for wrists, ankles, and neck. The top half slides into place and locks with a pair of pegs.

Pillory

The Pillory attachment and Stock turns base VersaHorse into a medieval-style pillory. Your partner's chest rests on a pad, with wrists and neck in the Stock. Ankles attach to eyebolts, resulting in a very secure yet surprisingly comfortable position for long play sessions.

Pegasus Bench

Named after the flying horse in Greek mythology, Pegasus Bench turns base VersaHorse into a flat bench with outriggers for your partner's arms and legs. Multiple straps attach arms to the wings, with legs attached at one of several outrigger levels.
