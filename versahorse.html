Stock

The stock dates back to Medieval times and is still with us today. Foxy Furniture's Stock is a classic design with holes for wrists, ankles, and neck. The top half slides into place and is locked down with a pair of pegs. Your partner can be placed in a variety of positions.

Pillory

The Pillory attachment uses the Stock and turns the base VersaHorse into a Medieval style pillory. Your partner's chest rests on a pad with wrists and neck in the Stock. Their ankles can be attached to eyebolts. The net effect is a very secure position - yet surprisingly comfortable for long play sessions.

Pegasus Bench

Named after the flying horse in Greek Mythology, the Pegasus Bench attachment turns the basic VersaHorse into a flat bench with outriggers for your partner's arms and legs. Multiple straps are used to affix arms to the "wings", with legs attached at one of several levels of the outriggers. The Headrests are removable so your partner's head can be lowered to afford access to their mouth and throat.
