About Us

Thanks for your interest in Foxy Furniture. Lady Hera and I founded the company in 2003 after a snag in Lady Hera's lingerie at a play party. We decided the world needed better-quality dungeon furniture, and we were just the people to deliver it. We've now retired, so we're giving the plans away instead of selling the furniture.

To reach us, use our contact form or DM DungeonToyMaker on FetLife.com.

If you're going to build...

The plans contain AutoCAD files, construction steps, and parts lists. They were originally just reminders for me, so allow time and patience to understand the plans, steps, and parts before you start cutting. The Gallery pages help too.

The PDF set contains the same drawings as PDFs. They are easier to use but cannot be modified.

To customize the drawings, you'll need an app that can handle AutoCAD (.dwg) files. Free options are available: just search for ".dwg editors." I use eDrawings on my Mac.

If you'd rather buy...

Fetish Furniture builds the VersaHorse and Criss-Cross designs. I haven't seen their work in person, but they get excellent reviews.
