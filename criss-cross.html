To transform from pedestal and plate shelf:

remove plate shelf,

roll Pedestal upside down,

retrieve plates from base bottom,

slide base off pedestal.

Pedestal column sides become cross legs:

assemble cross legs to plates,

attach bolts and wing nuts,

use hitch pins to afix cross to mounting bar.

Ready for play!

Criss Cross

The pedestal and display shelf make practical additions to your living space, yet easily transform into a St. Andrews Cross.

Criss-Cross is designed to be wall-mounted 78" above the floor. You can customize the height to fit your partner. The pedestal base is 14" square, and the standard pedestal height is 46".
